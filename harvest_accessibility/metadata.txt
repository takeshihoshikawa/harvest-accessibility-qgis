about=Given an operation area polygon, forest road lines and landing points, this plugin measures how far the wood has to travel: d1 from each tree to the road, and d2 along the road network to the landing that serves it. Sample points come from individual tree points where they exist, otherwise from a lattice. Supply a DEM -- or let the plugin fetch one -- and d1 becomes the distance from the road to the near end of the felled stem rather than to the standing tree, with felling direction limited by slope, by barriers such as rivers, and optionally by the block boundary. Outputs an HTML report plus per-tree layers: sample points with distances, hauling lines, and felled stems. Requires a projected CRS in metres.
